Content policy
Mintr — Community and Content Policy

1. What Mintr is

Mintr is a social and marketplace app. People talk, share, buy and sell. This policy covers what we remove, what we restrict, and why.

2. What we do not allow

  • Harassment, threats, or targeted abuse. Attacking someone because of who they are is not allowed.
  • Hate speech. Content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, age, or sexual orientation.
  • Nudity or sexual content involving minors. Zero tolerance. Immediate permanent ban and where legally required, reported to the authorities.
  • Spam, scams, or misleading financial offers. Fake giveaways, pyramid schemes, phishing links, impersonation for fraud.
  • Impersonation. Pretending to be a real person, brand, or business you are not.
  • Stolen goods or counterfeits. Listings for items you do not legally own or have the right to sell.
  • Violence or harm. Content celebrating or promoting violence against people or animals.
  • Sharing private information. Phone numbers, addresses, ID documents, or financial details of others without their consent.

3. What we allow with restriction

  • Adult content between consenting adults — restricted, not removed.
  • Strong opinions and sharp criticism — even uncomfortable ones — are fine.
  • Reporting on world events, including graphic imagery, when it serves the public interest.

4. How we act on reports

Every report is reviewed by a human. We can:

  • Dismiss the report — no action needed.
  • Restrict the content — visible to fewer people, no notification drama.
  • Remove the content — it disappears from Mintr.
  • Restrict the user — their reach is limited across the app.

5. Response time

We aim to review reports within 48 hours. For content involving immediate harm or minors, we act faster.

6. Restriction, not blocking

Mintr does not have blocking. We have restriction. You will not see content from people you have restricted, and they will not see yours. No dramatic confrontations, no need to tell anyone. It just works quietly.

7. Appeals

If your content was removed and you believe we got it wrong, reply to the removal notice. A human reviews every appeal.

8. Report something

Every post, profile, and chat has a report option. You can also reach us directly at mail@mintr-app.netlify.app.
